# Causal Inference for Policy Evaluation

[![License: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY--NC--ND%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/)

Open lecture notes on the methods used to evaluate policies and business decisions: randomized experiments, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity, fixed effects, difference-in-differences, synthetic control, and regression and matching. The notes are organized around one question, who or what decided who was treated, and each method is applied to recent case studies from leading journals in economics, finance, insurance, information systems, and operations management.

**Live site: [causal.feihuang.org](https://causal.feihuang.org)**

Developed by [Fei Huang](https://www.feihuang.org), UNSW Sydney.
